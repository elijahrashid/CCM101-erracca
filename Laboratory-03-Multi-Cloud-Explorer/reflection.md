# Mission Reflection

Out of the three cloud platforms, AWS impressed me the most, mainly because of how broad its service catalog is. Having over 200 services means there's almost always a purpose-built tool for whatever a business needs, and that breadth comes from AWS having the longest head start of the three providers.

At the same time, researching all three showed me how similar they really are underneath the branding. AWS, Azure, and GCP all offer the same basic building blocks — compute instances, object storage, managed databases, identity management, and Kubernetes — just under different names (EC2 vs. Azure VMs vs. Compute Engine, for example). All three also use pay-as-you-go pricing and are organized around regions and zones for global availability.

Working through the client recommendation scenarios made it clear that business requirements, not popularity, should drive the platform choice. A startup with a limited budget doesn't have the same needs as a university already running Windows Server and Active Directory, or an AI research company that needs GPU-heavy compute. Each client's existing tech stack, budget, and technical goals pointed toward a different "best" provider — which is exactly why a real cloud engineer has to evaluate the problem first instead of defaulting to whichever platform is most popular.

The biggest takeaway from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant was that mindset shift: it's not about picking the most popular platform, it's about matching the client's actual needs. That's a different way of thinking than just learning a tool — it's closer to how I'll actually need to make decisions in the field.

This lab also improved my GitHub portfolio in a concrete way — it added comparison tables and formal client-facing recommendations, which is a different style of documentation than the project READMEs and technical write-ups I usually do. It's a useful addition alongside my other repos, since it shows I can evaluate and compare options for a client, not just document a finished build.
